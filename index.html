3-Band Audio Equalizer

Analog Design RC Filters Op-Amps LTSpice Oscilloscope

Designed, constructed, and experimentally validated a three-band analog audio equalizer using passive RC filters, op-amp gain stages, signal recombination, and a power amplifier capable of driving an 8 Ω speaker. Verified frequency response from 100 Hz–10 kHz within ±2 dB across all bands.
