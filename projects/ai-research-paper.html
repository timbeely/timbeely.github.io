Project Overview

I authored a research paper examining the technical and ethical impact of artificial intelligence on engineering and business practices for Advanced Systems Engineering Corporation, a systems engineering and consulting company.

Technical Stack

  • Academic research and technical writing
  • Literature review of AI applications in engineering and business
  • Ethical analysis of machine learning systems
  • Systems engineering perspective on AI integration

Engineering Approach

  • Researched real-world AI implementations in engineering design, project management, and business operations.
  • Analyzed benefits such as automation, optimization, and predictive decision-making alongside ethical risks.
  • Evaluated case studies highlighting algorithmic bias, data integrity issues, and security vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaways

  • Strengthened my ability to evaluate emerging technologies from both technical and ethical perspectives.
  • Gained experience synthesizing information from technical literature, case studies, and industry examples into a structured engineering analysis.
  • Developed stronger technical-writing skills by communicating complex AI risks, benefits, and implementation considerations to an engineering audience.
